About This Website

FREE MATHEMATICS AND PHYSICS RESOURCES is an educational platform that provides accessible learning materials to students and teachers.

The focus is on the Ethiopian secondary school curriculum (Grades 9–12) and international university-level Mathematics and Physics (BSc/MSc).

Resources include textbooks, PDF notes, question banks, worked examples, exercises and interactive learning materials.
